.Procedure: Creating an access group
[role=procedure]
_____
. In the {webui}, navigate to menu:Admin[Access Control] and click btn:[Create Access Group].
. On the `Details` page, fill in the following fields:
* *Name*: Enter a unique name for the access group.
* *Description*: Enter a description explaining the purpose of the group.
* *Organization*: Select the organization to which the access group belongs.
* *Copy Permissions From*: Optionally select one or more existing access groups to inherit permissions from.
  Once created, the new group functions independently, and future changes to either group do not affect the other.
. Click btn:[Continue].
. On the `Namespaces & Permissions` page, select the permissions to grant to the access group:
* Select *View* to grant read-only access to a namespace.
* Select *Modify* to grant full access, including creating, updating, or deleting resources.
* Use the search bar (`Filter by name`) to locate specific namespaces.
* Filter permissions using the `API`, `Web`, and `Only selected` checkboxes.
* Click btn:[Expand All] or btn:[Collapse All] to expand or collapse all namespaces in the hierarchy.
. Click btn:[Continue].
. On the `Users` page, assign users to this access group:
* Select a user from the `Search & Add Users` dropdown to add them to the group.
* Review the list of assigned users in the table (showing `Username`, `Email`, and `Real Name`).
* To remove an assigned user, click the Delete icon in the `Actions` column.
. Click btn:[Continue].
. On the `Review` page, verify the access group details, assigned users, and granted permissions.
. Click btn:[Create] to finish creating the access group.
_____
