[IMPORTANT]
====
Upgrading to {productname} {productnumber} includes a {postgresql} major version upgrade from version 16 to 18. 
The migration process creates a full copy of the existing database in `_data_old` on the same filesystem. 

Ensure that the storage volume hosting `/var/lib/containers/storage/volumes/var-pgsql/` has at least twice the free space of your current database size before starting `mgradm migration` or `mgradm upgrade`.
====
